# Vacancy Scoring

Agent: calibrate this private file against the candidate's confirmed priorities
and constraints. Keep the public dimensions as a starting point, record every
candidate-specific change, and never reward an unsupported claim.

Use a 0–100 score and keep strategic fit separate from practical risk.

## Base dimensions

- Mandate and decision authority: 0–25
- Match to verified experience: 0–25
- Scope, ownership, and growth opportunity: 0–20
- Domain and motivation fit: 0–15
- Compensation and practical fit: 0–15

## Calibration

Define explicit caps for hard mismatches. A prestigious title must not override
a missing must-have, an excluded task, an ethical conflict, or an impossible
location requirement. Record every cap and penalty in the vacancy reason/risks.

## Decision bands

- Below 70: reject or archive with a reason.
- 70–84: review or resolve open questions.
- 85–100: recommend applying if all facts are confirmed.

The effective apply threshold and whether automatic submission is allowed come
from `config/settings.toml`, not from this example.
